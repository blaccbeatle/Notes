Gestational Diabetes
Booking (<12 weeks)
  → FBS (preferred) or PPBS
        │
        ├─ Normal → 2nd screen
        │           at 24–28 weeks
        │
        └─ Suspicious
             → OGTT confirms
             → likely PRE-EXISTING DM
               → monitor with BSS

24–28 weeks: 2nd screen
        │
        ├─ Risk factors present
        │     → OGTT (75 g; fasting,
        │       1 h, 2 h = 3 samples)
        └─ No risk factors → no OGTT
        │
        ▼
GDM confirmed → HIGH-RISK
  ├─ More frequent visits
  ├─ Kick count at 32–34 weeks
  ├─ Control sugar early
  │   (≈35–36 wks too late to
  │    limit growth)
  ├─ Insulin not mandatory
  └─ Deliver 37–38 weeks
        │
        ▼

Family Planning
Where to get
  ├─ Domiciliary → COCP + condoms
  ├─ MOH → all temporary except
  │        POP, Mirena; no permanent
  ├─ FHB → POP, Mirena not avaliable now
  └─ Vasectomy → hospital

Starting a hormonal method
  Day 1–5 of cycle?
    ├─ Yes → immediate protection
    └─ No → barrier for 7 days

Missed COCP
  ├─ 1 pill → take ASAP, continue
  └─ ≥2 pills → barrier 7 days
       ├─ ≥7 active left → same pack
       └─ <7 left → skip placebo,
          start new pack

Emergency contraception
  ├─ LNG 0.75 mg × 2 (12 h apart)
  │  or 1.5 mg → within 72 h
  └─ Cu IUCD → within 5 days

  ├─ Cu IUCD → ≤48 h, else 6 wks
  ├─ LRT → ≤7 days, else 6 wks
  ├─ COCP → 6 months

        ▼

IMMEDIATELY
        │
        ▼

  → MOH, RDHS, PDHS,
    Director FHB, previous head
        │
        ▼

 PHM, MOH, RDHS, MO MCH)
        │
        ▼
H677 report within 14 days
  → head, RDHS, Director FHB
        │
        ▼
Purpose: correct errors
(NOT legal action)

Classification
  ├─ ≤42 days → maternal death
  │   (used for MMR)
  │    ├─ Direct (obstetric)
  │    └─ Indirect (disease worsened)
