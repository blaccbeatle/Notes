9. PUBLIC vs PRIVATE

Government
  -> everything free
  -> medical certificate:
     govt servants: free
     private sector: Rs. 50

Private
  -> everything charged

Outpatient
  ~45% govt / ~55% private
Inpatient
  ~96% govt / ~4% private

---

Government -> Western, Ayurvedic
Private -> + Unani, Siddha,
           Homoeopathy,
           Acupuncture

Ayurvedic <-X-> Western drugs
  -> lack of training

AMP/RMO
  -> non-MBBS ("apothecary")
  -> doctor shortage during
     high malaria prevalence

Accessibility
  -> 4.8 km: nearest govt
     Western facility
  -> 1.4 km: any health service

Leading causes of death
  -> IHD + cerebrovascular



10. HEALTHY LIFESTYLE CENTRES

>=2 per MOH area
  |
  +-- Group A
  |     healthy >=35 years
  |     -> every 3 years
  |
  `-- Group B
        20-34 + risk factors
        -> annually

Risk factors
  -> smoking (>=1 year)
  -> BMI >=25
  -> waist >90 cm (M)
           >80 cm (F)
  -> BP >=140/90
  -> diabetes symptoms
  -> premature CVD in 1st-degree
     (father <55, mother <65)
  -> family diabetes
  -> familial dyslipidaemia

Investigations
  -> FBS/RBS
  -> total cholesterol
  -> NO routine thyroid



11. CASE EXAMPLES

Asthma at PMCU
  -> nebulise (all hospitals)
  -> oral steroids
  -> avoid IV hydrocortisone

Notification
  -> MOH of residence/work,
     not hometown
  -> death in other district
     -> both RDHS + both PDHS

Septic shock, 20 weeks
  -> LSCS: Base B+
  -> no improvement -> ICU
