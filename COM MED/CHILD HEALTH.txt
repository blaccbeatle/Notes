Micronutrient Deficiencies
IRON
 -> hypochromic microcytic
 Commonest; ~1/3 pregnant
 Highest: estate sector
 (tea tannins, hookworm,
  low SES)
 Absorption UP:
  vit C, heme iron
 Absorption DOWN:
  tea, coffee, phytates,
  calcium

FOLATE / B12
 -> megaloblastic anaemia
 Folate -> NTDs
 Give 3 mo pre-conception

IODINE
 -> goitre, cretinism, MR,
    abortion, IUD
 Easy indicator: goitre
 Hard: urinary iodine (precise)
 Prevent: iodised salt
