GENERAL POISONING

  |
  +--> Accidental
  +--> Suicidal
  +--> Homicidal

Common in Sri Lanka
  |
  +--> OP
  +--> Kaneru
         |
         v

  |             |

  |      assess build, injuries
  |      (or mixed in food/drink,
  |       victim made drunk first)
  |
  +--> Inhalation / dermal
  |      |
  |      v

  |
  +--> Local (skin, mucosa)
  +--> Systemic
  +--> Specific organ
  |      +--> liver & kidney
  |      |    (commonest)
  |      +--> lung (paraquat)
  +--> Cellular (cyanide)

---

  |
  +--> Poison: type, route,
  |    physical form
  +--> Host: empty stomach,
  |    extremes of age,
  |    tolerance, idiosyncrasy
  +--> Interaction: synergism,

---

History clues
  |
  +--> previous attempts
  +--> suicide note
  +--> anaesthetists
  |    (access + knowledge)
  +--> irritant poison
         |
         v

---

  |      --> ARSENIC
  +--> Burton lines --> LEAD
  +--> Jaundice --> alcohol,
  |    phosphorus
  +--> Bright red --> CYANIDE
  +--> Pink --> CO

GASTRIC LAVAGE AND EMESIS

Gastric lavage
  |
  v
within 1 hour
  |
  +--> NOT if:
  |      +--> time unknown
  |      +--> drowsy (aspiration)
  |      +--> corrosives
  |           (e.g. kerosene)
  |
  +--> CAN do: datura

---

Induced emesis
  |
  +--> CONTRAINDICATED:
  |      |
  |      +--> kerosene
  |      |      --> chemical
  |      |          pneumonitis
  |      +--> strong acid/alkali
  |      |      --> perforation
  |      +--> unconscious
  |      |      --> aspiration
  |      +--> CNS stimulants
  |             --> convulsions
  |
  +--> NOT contraindicated:
       paracetamol



ALCOHOL

Absorption
(stomach + small intestine)
  |
  +--> Delayed:
  |    food (fat/protein)
  |      --> slow gastric
  |          emptying
  |
  +--> Faster:
  |    carbonated, low conc.
  |
  +--> High conc.
         --> pylorospasm
         --> slower

---

Distribution: body water only
  |
  v
Females (more fat, less water)
  |
  v
higher BAC

Metabolism: ~90% liver

Death: medullary paralysis
  --> respiratory arrest

Flushing: cutaneous
vasodilation

---

BAC stages
  |
  v
50-100 mg%
  euphoria, talkative,
  loss of self-control
  |
  v
100-200 mg%
  confusion, slow reaction,
  incoordination, slurred
  speech
  |
  v
>200 mg%
  stupor, coma,
  resp. depression
  |
  v
>500 --> death

---

Driving
  |
  +--> overconfidence
  +--> tunnel vision
  +--> delayed reaction
  +--> impaired colour vision,
  |    accommodation
  +--> steering sway,
       centre-road tendency

---

Legal limit (Sri Lanka)
  |
  v
80 mg% (80 mg/100 ml blood)

Balloon breathalyser
  |
  v
potassium dichromate
+ sulphuric acid
  |
  v
alcohol --> acetic acid
  |
  v
yellow --> green
(only >80 mg%)
  |
  +--> police; once only;
  |    one officer enough
  +--> false +ve: asthma/
  |    COPD inhalers
  +--> not specific
         --> challengeable
             in court

---

Blood test
  |
  v
no routine legal provision
  |
  v

  |
  +--> JMO cannot order on
  |    non-consenting patient
  +--> Magistrate order
         --> no consent needed
         --> clinicians bound

---

  |

---

ORGANOPHOSPHATES

Insecticide (phenthoate,
malathion)
  |
  v
commonest suicidal poison
in SL (~33%)

Mechanism
  |
  v
inhibits acetylcholinesterase
at NMJ

Much larger amount needed
to cause death

---

Clinical
  |
  +--> Muscarinic (DUMBBELLS)
  |      diarrhoea, urination,
  |      MIOSIS, BRADYCARDIA,
  |      bronchospasm/
  |      bronchorrhoea, emesis,
  |      lacrimation, salivation
  |
  +--> Nicotinic
  |      fasciculations,
  |      weakness, paralysis
  |
  +--> Intermediate syndrome
         |
         v
       ~24-96 hours to 4 days
         |
         v
       resp. muscle paralysis
         |
         v
       death
       (treat: pralidoxime)

---

Management (within 1-2 hrs)
  |
  v
activated charcoal
  |
  v
remove clothes
  |
  v
atropine infusion (large)
  |
  v
pralidoxime
  |
  v
intubate if resp. depression
+ bronchospasm

Atropine: CANNOT fix weakness
Pralidoxime: CAN fix weakness

---

Autopsy
  |
  +--> kerosene-like smell
  +--> oily green stomach
  +--> heavy lungs
  +--> flame-shaped sub-
  |    endocardial haemorrhages
  +--> NO lung fibrosis
  +--> no specific signs



PARAQUAT

Herbicide (Gramoxone)
blue-green, repulsive smell
  |
  v
lethal: 5 ml (1 teaspoon)
(very small amount lethal)
  |
  v
poor GI absorption (~5-10%)

---

Early destructive
(within 24 hrs)
  |
  +--> corrosive burns
  +--> CARDIOTOXICITY --> shock
  +--> ATN
  +--> liver necrosis
  |
  v
Late proliferative
(~7 days onward)
  |
  v
progressive dyspnoea
  |
  v
PARAQUAT LUNG
(diffuse interstitial/
intra-alveolar fibrosis)
  |
  v
lungs heavier by ~1 week

---

Autopsy
  |
  +--> blue-green organs
  +--> solid, rubbery lungs
  +--> honeycomb cut section



SUBSTANCE ABUSE

Cocaine / amphetamines
("fight-or-flight")
  |
  v
increased HR, BP, RR
sweating, dilated pupils,
hyperthermia
  |
  v
death: arrhythmia, MI,
stroke, serotonin syndrome

Cocaine
  |
  +--> most potent natural
  |    CNS stimulant
  +--> enhanced senses
  |      --> minor stimuli
  |          seen as threats
  |      --> PARANOIA
  +--> coronary spasm --> MI
  +--> pregnancy: foetal death,
       abortion, abruption

Amphetamines / MDMA
  |
  +--> longer effect
  +--> serotonin syndrome
  +--> fulminant liver failure
  +--> cerebral oedema
       (worse w/ hyponatraemia)

---

Opioids ("calmer world")
  |
  v
decreased HR, BP, RR
PINPOINT pupils,
hypothermia
  |
  v
death: RESP. DEPRESSION,
pulmonary oedema

Fentanyl: ~100 times more
potent than morphine

---

Cannabis
  |
  v
tachycardia, dry mouth,
normal pupils/red eyes
  |
  v
NO direct death

---

Heroin withdrawal
("when someone you love
  leaves you")
  |
  v

rhinorrhoea, lacrimation,
yawning, anorexia,

depression, incontinence



THERAPEUTIC DRUG OVERDOSE

Aspirin
  |
  v
slow absorption
+ pylorospasm
  |
  v
EARLY: resp. alkalosis
(brain stimulation
 --> hyperventilation)
  |
  v
LATER: high anion gap
metabolic (lactic) acidosis
  |
  +--> tinnitus
  +--> children: CNS effects
  |
  v
Rx: NaHCO3 urine
alkalinisation, haemodialysis
(NaHCO3 = treatment,
 NOT antidote)
  |
  v
NO specific antidote
  |
  v
PM: gastric erosions,
petechiae, pulmonary/
cerebral oedema

Ix: salicylate levels;
ferric chloride urine test

---

Paracetamol
  |
  v
fast absorption
  |
  v
toxic metabolite: NAPQI
  |
  v
hepatotoxicity
--> hepatorenal
  |
  v
Rx: lavage + charcoal
--> IV NAC
(intolerant --> oral

  |
  v

proximal tubular necrosis

Ix: blood level vs nomogram;

---

TCAs

  v

---

CYANIDE

Mechanism
  |
  v
HISTOTOXIC hypoxia
(cells cannot use O2
 despite delivery)

Circumstance
  |
  +--> jewellery workers,
  |    terrorists
  +--> gas --> instant death

---

PM
  |
  +--> BRICK RED / bright red
  +--> ALMOND smell
  +--> red froth
  +--> oral burns

Rx
  |
  +--> IV hydroxocobalamin
  +--> sodium thiosulphate
  +--> dicobalt edetate
  +--> amyl nitrite
  +--> NO emesis

Samples
  |
  +--> lungs in NYLON BAG
  |    tied at hilum
  +--> 10 ml peripheral blood
       in NaF



CARBON MONOXIDE

Circumstance
  |
  +--> closed garage
  |    (accidental)
  +--> exhaust (suicidal)

Two actions
  |
  +--> high Hb affinity
  |      --> hypoxia
  +--> direct heart/brain
       toxicity

---

COHb levels
  |
  v
<5%: normal
(higher in SMOKERS)
  |
  v
~30%: cherry pink
  |
  v
40%: nausea, tachycardia
  |
  v
40-60%: confusion
  |
  v
60%: unconscious, seizures

Breathing: Cheyne-Stokes

---

Rx
  |
  +--> 100% O2 (main)
  +--> hyperbaric O2
  |    (pressure displaces
  |     bound CO)
  +--> transfusion

PM: PINK; COHb detectable
even when putrefied

Colour clue:
red --> cyanide
pink --> CO

HIGH-YIELD POINTS

- Legal limit 80 mg%;
  death >500 mg%
- Balloon yellow --> green;
  inhalers --> false +ve
- Blood test: Magistrate only;
  HCW suspect --> 2 people
- PM alcohol: 1% NaF,
  peripheral vein
- OP: ~33% suicides; kerosene
  smell; atropine +
  pralidoxime
- Paraquat: 5 ml fatal;
  fibrosis ~7 days
- Paracetamol --> NAPQI
  --> centrilobular; NAC
- Aspirin, TCA: NaHCO3 =
  treatment, no antidote
- Arsenic: Mees', raindrop,
  garlic, red velvet
- Lead: Burton's, stippling,
  wrist drop
- BAL all; penicillamine
  Hg + Cu only
- Kaneru: yellow vision,
  AV blocks
- Datura --> physostigmine
- Cyanide: brick red, almond
- CO: pink, 100% O2
- Lavage within 1 hour
