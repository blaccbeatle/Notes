KEY TIMELINES: FORENSIC NOTES
=============================

1. TIME SINCE DEATH

AT DEATH
  Cadaveric spasm: instant
  (freezes the last act)

---
COOLING (algor mortis)
  Rectal temp every ~30 min
  over ~6 h
  Not usable in first hour
  |
  v
  Plateau ~1-3 h
  |
  v
  Fast drop ~6 h
  USE THIS PHASE TO CALCULATE
  |
  v
  Slow fall
  |
  v
  Equilibrium with environment

---
FORMULA (stage 2 cooling)
  TSD = (98.4F - rectal temp)
        / cooling rate (F/h)

---
HYPOSTASIS
  ~30 min: reddish blotches
  |
  v
  4-6 h: well-developed
  |
  v
  after ~12 h: FIXED
  
  Position change:
    <6 h    shifts to new site
    6-12 h  present in both
    >12 h   fixed in original

---
RIGOR MORTIS
  Starts ~2 h (all muscles
  at once, detected first
  in small muscles)
  |
  v
  Order of detection:
  ~2 h    jaw
  2-4 h   face/neck
  4-6 h   trunk
  6-8 h   large joints
  8-10 h  small joints
  |
  v
  Complete ~6-12 h
  |
  v
  Persists ~12-24 h
  |
  v
  Secondary flaccidity
  |
  v
  Decomposition begins
  24-36 h

  Quicker onset in infants

---
EYES AND STOMACH
  Cornea:
    4-6 h    lacy/cloudy
    8-12 h   dull/opaque
  Tache noire:
    normally >12 h
    sometimes 4-6 h
  Stomach:
    empty = ~6-8 h after
    last meal

---
USE
  Cooling + rigor = first ~24 h
  Later changes = longer periods
  Cooling = most reliable
  Rigor = 2nd
  Hypostasis = least of the three



2. PUTREFACTION (WARM, AVERAGE)

  ~18 h   green, right iliac fossa
   |
   v
  ~24 h   green spreads to abdomen
          + gas distension
   |
   v
  ~36 h   marbling
   |
   v
  ~48 h   puffy face, eyelids,
          genitalia
   |
   v
  ~72 h   whole body distended
   |
   v
  ~4 days glove & stocking
   |
   v
  3-4 wk  soft tissue gone,
          skeleton exposed
   |
   v
  3 mo-1 yr  full skeletonization
             + disarticulation

---
ALTERNATIVE
  Adipocere: whole body ~1 year



3. INJURY DATING

BRUISE COLOUR
  Immediate   red
   |
   v
  1-2 days    blue/purple
   |
   v
  2-4 days    black/bluish-black
   |
   v
  4-6 days    green
   |
   v
  6-8 days    yellow
   |
   v
  Fades within 1-2 weeks
  (10-14 days)
  Elderly: weeks to a couple
  of months (Perls' stain)

---
ABRASION
  ~10 min   clotting
   |
   v
  ~30 min   neutrophils
   |
   v
  ~24 h     dry scab
  Fades within 1-2 weeks
  "1 week" = accepted true
  "1-2 weeks" = not accepted

---
BURNS (aging)
  0-12 h    moist exudate
   |
   v
  12-24 h   dry exudate
   |
   v
  24-36 h   brown crust
   |
   v
  36-48 h to ~4 days
            inflammation gone
   |
   v
  ~2 weeks  superficial slough
            falls off
   |
   v
  Few weeks deep slough falls off
   |
   v
  Scarring

---
WOUND HEALING
  0 min        hemostasis
   |
   v
  30 min-1 hr  neutrophils
               (peak 24-48 hrs)
   |
   v
  24-48 hrs    macrophages
               (dominant 48-72 hrs)
   |
   v
  48-72 hrs    fibroblasts
               (days 3-5)
   |
   v
  Day 3        proliferative
               (through day 21)
   |
   v
  Weeks        remodeling
               (up to 1-2 years)

---
FRACTURE HEALING
  12-36 h    haematoma
   |
   v
  ~2-3 wk    callus
   |
   v
  ~2-4 mo    consolidation
   |
   v
  Many months remodelling

  Upper limb: 3 weeks
  Lower limb: 6 weeks
  "Heals within 6 weeks" = TRUE
  (may extend 6 wk-3 mo)
  "Heals within 1 month" = FALSE

---
SUBDURAL HAEMORRHAGE
  Acute      up to ~48 hours
  Subacute   2-14 days
  Chronic    2-3 weeks
             (months possible)



4. MEDICO-LEGAL

GRIEVOUS HURT (I)
  Severe pain / unable to
  follow ordinary pursuits
  = 20 DAYS
  |
  v
  Review after 3 weeks
  (grievous E and I)

---
MATERNAL DEATH
  Pregnancy -> delivery ->
  puerperium (6 weeks
  postpartum)

---
MEDICAL CERTIFICATES
  Viral fever:
    1 day usually enough
    3 days if needed
    7 days NOT justified
  ERPC:           3-5 days
  Small abscess:  3 days
  Major surgery:  3-4 weeks
                  minimum
  20 days -> consider
  grievous hurt



5. DROWNING, SEXUAL, ABORTION

WASHERWOMAN'S HANDS
  Fingertips: ~2 h
  Whole hand: 8-12 h

---
SPERM IN VAGINA
  Motile complete: up to 16 h
  Dead complete:   up to 24 h
  Fragmented:      up to 4 days

---
ABORTION / GESTATION
  Conception to 22 wk
    = spontaneous abortion
  22-28 wk  early foetal death
  28 wk to birth
            late foetal death

  Methods by gestation:
  ~5 wk    syringe aspiration
  ~12 wk+  suction termination
  >14 wk   hysterotomy etc.



6. TOXICOLOGY

  Alcohol:
    absorbed within 2 hours

---
ORGANOPHOSPHATE
  Management within 1-2 hrs
  |
  v
  Intermediate syndrome
  ~24-96 h to 4 days

---
PARAQUAT
  Within ~24 h
  early destructive phase
  |
  v
  ~7 days onward
  late proliferative phase



7. AGE ESTIMATION

TEETH
  Primary erupt:
    ~6 months-2 years
  Permanent erupt:
    ~6 years to 24-25 years
  After ~25 years:
    UNRELIABLE

---
CRANIAL SUTURES
  Sagittal:
    begins early 20s
    complete by 30-40
  Coronal, lambdoid:
    closure after 30
  Whole vault: 30-60+ years
