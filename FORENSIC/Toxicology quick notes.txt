High-Yield Points

	•	Arsenic → diarrhoea; lead → constipation
	•	Rice water diarrhoea: arsenic and niyangala (mimics cholera)
	•	Lines:
	•	Burton lines (gums) → lead
	•	Brown-blue lines (gums) → mercury
	•	Mees lines (nails) → arsenic
	•	Nerves: arsenic → neuropathy; lead → wrist and foot drop; mercury → neuritis
	•	Mercury → distal GIT, nerve, and kidney inflammation
	•	Cerebellar signs: benzodiazepines and mercury
	•	TCA → seizures
	•	Benzodiazepines and barbiturates → drowsiness, confusion, coma
	•	Liver and kidney necrosis: paracetamol, paraquat (early), castor
	•	Subendocardial haemorrhages: OP and arsenic
	•	PM hypoxic changes: barbiturates and opioids
	•	NaF preserves samples for cyanide and alcohol
	•	Plant poisoning → look for seeds or plant material at PM
	•	Poisoning in children → usually accidental
