PELVIC PAIN NOTES
=================


OBSTETRICS VS GYNAECOLOGY

Women's health
  |
  |-> normal physiology
  |     (pregnancy, birth)
  |     -> OBSTETRICS
  |
  |-> deviation = pathology
        -> GYNAECOLOGY

---

Examples

Failure to conceive
  -> subfertility -> gynae

Normal bleeding -> physiology

Too much / too little bleeding
  -> pathology -> gynae

DYSMENORRHEA

Exaggerated period pain
that affects quality of life
  |
  v
Which type?
  |
  |-> PRIMARY
  |     exaggerated physiology
  |     no disease
  |     starts with bleeding
  |     ends when bleeding stops
  |
  |-> SECONDARY
        underlying pelvic disease
        starts before, with
        or after period
        may last 1-2 days after

---

Causes of secondary

  endometriosis
  adenomyosis
  PID
  pelvic congestion syndrome

---

KEY RULE
Pain confined to the period
Between periods
  -> NOT dysmenorrhea


CHRONIC PELVIC PAIN (CPP)

Pelvic pain > 6 months
  |
  v
Patterns
  |-> continuous (all day)
  |-> intermittent (comes, goes)
  |-> cyclical (worse in menses)
  |-> non-cyclical (no link)

---

Main cause of cyclical CPP
  -> pelvic congestion syndrome


PELVIC CONGESTION SYNDROME

Anatomy
  |
  |-> LEFT ovarian vein
  |     -> left renal vein
  |     right angle
  |     HIGH risk of incompetence
  |
  |-> RIGHT ovarian vein
        -> IVC
        oblique angle
        LOWER risk

---

Mechanism

venous incompetence
  |
  v
blood congests around ovary
  |
  v
PAIN
  |
  v
worse in menses
(more pelvic blood flow)

Left side more common
Right side can occur

---

Clinical clue

Standing
  -> more congestion
  -> pain WORSE

Lying down
  -> better venous return
  -> pain BETTER


NON-CYCLICAL CPP

Pain between periods
(intermittent or continuous)
  |
  v
Causes
  |
  |-> PID
  |     infection, inflammation,
  |     edema
  |     may worsen in menses
  |
  |-> adhesions
  |     post-infection
  |     post-surgery
  |
  |-> nerve entrapment
  |
  |-> inflammatory bowel disease
  |
  |-> IBS
  |     pain often after eating
  |
  |-> psychological
  |     no organic pathology
  |     pain still real
  |
  |-> bladder pain syndrome
        bladder pain, dysuria,
        urgency
        cause unknown

---

Contrast

Secondary dysmenorrhea
  -> pain mostly in the period

Non-cyclical pain
  -> also between periods

All CPP forms
  -> reduce quality of life
