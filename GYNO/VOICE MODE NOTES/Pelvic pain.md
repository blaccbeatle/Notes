DYSMENORRHOEA

Exaggerated period pain
that hurts quality of life
  |
  +-- no disease
  |     |
  |     v
  |   PRIMARY
  |   starts with bleeding
  |   ends when it stops
  |
  +-- pelvic disease
        |
        v
      SECONDARY

---

SECONDARY CAUSES

  - Endometriosis
  - Adenomyosis
  - PID
  - Pelvic congestion

Timing: before, with or
after period; may last
1-2 days after it stops

---

KEY RULE

Pain confined to the
period only
  |
  v
Between periods
= NOT dysmenorrhoea

---

(ADD) Treat primary with
NSAIDs or hormonal
contraception
