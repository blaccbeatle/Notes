INSTRUMENTAL DELIVERY
=====================


BASICS

2nd stage, full dilatation
  |
  v
Baby needs help to deliver
  |
  v
Forceps or Vacuum

---

Why assist?
  |
  +-> Labour: prolonged 2nd stage
  |   (commonest)
  +-> Foetus: distress
  +-> Mother: cannot push well
        |
        +-> exhaustion
        +-> severe hypertension
        +-> heart disease
        +-> myasthenia gravis
        +-> spinal cord injury
        +-> proliferative retinopathy



CLASSIFICATION

Where is the head?
  |
  +-> Above ischial spines
  |     -> HIGH: no instruments
  +-> Spines to +2
  |     -> MID
  +-> Below +2
  |     -> LOW
  +-> On perineum, scalp seen
        without parting labia
        -> OUTLET

---

Which instrument?
  |
  +-> Vacuum: outlet, low, mid
  +-> Forceps: type depends
        on cavity



PREREQUISITES

All must be YES
  |
  +-> Cervix fully dilated
  +-> Membranes ruptured
  +-> Position known
  +-> Station at/below spines
  +-> Head 1/5 or less palpable
  |
  v
Any NO -> do not proceed



CONTRAINDICATIONS

General
  |
  +-> Prerequisites not met
  +-> Suspected CPD

---

Forceps
  |
  +-> Occipito-transverse
  |     -> rotate to OA first
  +-> Osteogenesis imperfecta
        -> risk head-body
           separation

---

Vacuum
  |
  +-> Preterm < 34 weeks
  |     -> intracranial bleed
  +-> Maternal thrombocytopenia
  |     -> baby may have it
  +-> Face presentation
        -> eyeball prolapse



FORCEPS

Pick the type
  |
  +-> Outlet
  |     -> Wrigley's
  +-> Low or mid cavity
  |     -> Simpson's (finger guard)
  |     -> Neville-Barnes
  +-> Occipito-transverse
        -> rotate to OA first
        -> Kielland's (rarely
           used, high morbidity)

---

Technique
  |
  v
Episiotomy (always)
  |
  v
Pencil grip, parallel to
opposite inguinal ligament
  |
  v
Right blade first
  |
  v
Left blade, then lock
  |
  v
Traction DOWN (flexes head)
  |
  v
At introitus: traction UP
(extends head)
  |
  v
Path = J shape

---

Key facts
  |
  +-> Maternal-damaging
  +-> Foetal-friendly
  +-> No need to sync with
  |   contractions
  +-> Face marks resolve
        in 4-5 days



VACUUM

Find the flexion point
  |
  v
Vaginal exam: find posterior
fontanelle (triangle)
  |
  v
Measure 3 cm anterior along
sagittal suture
  |
  v
Centre cup over that point

---

Apply
  |
  v
Check no maternal tissue caught
  |
  v
Build to 600 mmHg
  |
  v
Wait for contraction
  |
  v
Mother pushes + you pull
(down, then up in J at crowning)
  |
  v
Chignon settles by itself

---

Key facts
  |
  +-> Maternal-friendly
  +-> Baby-risky
  |     |
  |     +-> intracranial bleed
  |     +-> subgaleal bleed
  |     +-> subarachnoid bleed
  +-> Weaker than forceps
        -> MUST sync with
           contractions + pushing



ABANDON AND GO TO CAESAREAN

Stop if ANY of these
  |
  +-> No descent after one
  |   moderate pull
  +-> More than 2 pop-offs
  +-> No delivery within
        20 minutes
