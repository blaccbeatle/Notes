CAESAREAN TYPES

Type of caesarean
  |
  +-- Lower segment (preferred)
  |     fibrous, less bleeding
  |     rupture risk ~0.5%
  |     VBAC possible
  |
  +-- Upper (classical)
        rupture risk ~5%
        VBAC not recommended

DECIDING MODE OF DELIVERY

Overall VBAC success ~75%
  |
  v
~90% if previous vaginal birth

---

Favourable
  |
  +-- non-recurring indication
  +-- younger age
  +-- smaller baby
  +-- engaged head
  +-- spontaneous labour

---

Unfavourable
  |
  +-- recurring indication
  |   (CPD, uterine anomaly)
  +-- age 40 or over
  +-- large baby
  +-- induction

BENEFITS AND RISKS

VBAC
  |
  +-- Benefits
  |     no surgery
  |     shorter stay
  |     future vaginal births
  |     less neonatal resp distress
  |     no family size limit
  |
  +-- Risks
        rupture ~0.5%
        instrumental delivery ~39%
        tears
        higher endometritis

---

Repeat caesarean
  |
  +-- Benefits
  |     planned
  |     lower rupture risk
  |     lower transfusion risk
  |     protects pelvic floor
  |     allows sterilisation
  |
  +-- Risks
        adhesions
        organ injury
        longer recovery
        more previa and PPH
        wound infection
        anaesthesia complications



CONTRAINDICATIONS

Avoid VBAC if
  |
  +-- previous rupture
  +-- previous classical caesarean
  |     (rupture risk 2-9%)
  +-- 3 or more caesareans
  +-- placenta previa
  +-- lower segment or
      cervical fibroids

---

Practice differs
  |
  +-- Sri Lanka: after 1 caesarean
  |
  +-- Western: after 2 caesareans
