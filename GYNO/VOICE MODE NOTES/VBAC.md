VBAC EXAM FLOWCHARTS
====================


BASICS AND CS BACKGROUND

VBAC = vaginal birth after CS
(trial of scar / trial of labour)
  |
  v
Goal: fewer repeat CS,
fewer complications,
positive birth experience

---

Layers cut (repair = reverse):

skin ->
subcut tissue ->
rectus sheath ->
peritoneum ->
visceral peritoneum ->
uterus ->
amniotic membrane

---

Indications (commonest first):

fetal distress
  |
previous CS
  |
placenta previa
  |
lower segment fibroid
  |
severe heart disease / PHT
  |
HIV (some cases)
  |
maternal request



TYPES AND COMPLICATIONS

Which incision?
  |
  +-- LOWER SEGMENT (preferred)
  |     forms after 28 wks
  |     fibrous, few vessels
  |     less bleeding
  |     rupture ~0.5% (1:200)
  |
  +-- UPPER / CLASSICAL
        more muscle + bleeding
        rupture ~5% (10x)
        NO VBAC

---

Upper segment used when:
  |
  +-- extreme emergency
  |
  +-- lower segment problem
        bladder adhesion
        lower fibroid
        previa

---

Complications:
  |
  +-- immediate
  |     bleeding, pain
  |     anaesthetic problems
  |     bladder/bowel injury
  |
  +-- intermediate
  |     infection
  |     endometriosis
  |     readmission
  |
  +-- long-term
        hernia
        risk up with repeats

---

Previa over scar
  |
  v
adherent placenta
  |
  v
massive bleeding
  |
  v
hysterectomy

BENEFITS AND RISKS

VBAC BENEFITS
  avoids surgery
  discharge next day
    (CS: 2-3 days)
  future vaginal birth
  no family size limit
  less neonatal RDS
    (labour stress ->
     glucocorticoids ->
     surfactant)
  lower maternal morbidity

---

REPEAT CS BENEFITS
  planned
  less transfusion
  less endometritis
  no rupture risk
  protects pelvic floor
  sterilisation possible

---

VBAC RISKS
  rupture ~0.5%
  emergency CS
  instrumental ~39%
    (vs 10-15%)
  sphincter damage, tears
  transfusion
  endometritis

---

REPEAT CS RISKS
  adhesions
    -> bladder/bowel injury
  long recovery
  more previa, adherent
    placenta, PPH
  wound infection
  anaesthetic problems
    (spinal headache,
     meningitis)



CONTRAINDICATIONS

Previous rupture?
  |
  yes -> NO VBAC

Previous classical CS?
(rupture 2-9%)
  |
  yes -> NO VBAC

3+ previous CS?
  |
  yes -> NO VBAC

---

Number of previous CS
  |
  +-- Sri Lanka: only 1
  |
  +-- West: up to 2

---

Other contraindications:
  placenta previa
  lower segment fibroid
  cervical fibroid



LABOUR MANAGEMENT

High risk -> close monitoring
(rupture = massive bleed,
 death possible)

---

Suspected rupture
  |
  v
immediate theatre
  |
  +-- repairable -> repair
  |
  +-- not -> hysterectomy

---

Induction
  |
  +-- OK: ARM, Foley
  |
  +-- NO: prostaglandins
  |     (lyse scar)
  |

---

Rupture signs
  FHR changes
    (first + most consistent)
  continuous pain / pain
    between contractions
  vaginal bleeding,
    haematuria
  palpable fetal parts
  contractions stop
  maternal tachycardia
    -> shock
  blood in liquor
