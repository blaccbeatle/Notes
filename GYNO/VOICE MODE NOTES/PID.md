PID: CONSOLIDATED FLOWCHARTS
===========================

PATHOGENESIS

Infection in vagina
  |
  v
Cervix (cervicitis)
  |
  v
Endometrium (endometritis)
  |
  v
Fallopian tubes (salpingitis)
  |
  v
Ovaries (oophoritis)
  |
  v
Tubo-ovarian abscess
  |
  v
Peritoneum (peritonitis)

---

Why this is possible:
Tract is open from vagina
straight to peritoneum



DEFENSES AND RISK FACTORS

6 natural defenses:
  - acidic vaginal pH (~4)
  - normally closed cervix
  - cervical mucous plug
  - IgA in cervical mucus
  - intact hymen
  - downward ciliary movement
  |
  v
Breached by:

---

Mechanical / obstetric:
  - instrumentation
    (D&C, HSG, biopsy)
  - IUCD
  - pregnancy, delivery,
    abortion

---

Infective / host:
  - STIs
  - immunosuppression

---

Drug effect:
  Broad-spectrum antibiotics
  |
  v
  Kill lactobacilli
  |
  v
  Vaginal pH rises
  |
  v
  Ascent easier



CLINICAL PICTURE

PID
  |
  +-- ACUTE
  |     sudden pain
  |     purulent discharge
  |     fever, sepsis signs
  |     dyspareunia
  |     cervical motion
  |     tenderness (classic)
  |
  +-- CHRONIC
        follows unresolved
        acute disease
        ill-looking, malnourished
        chronic pelvic pain
        back pain
        subfertility
        menorrhagia

---

Untreated / unresolved
  |
  v
Adhesions + fibrosis
  |
  v
Tubal occlusion
  |
  +-> subfertility
  +-> chronic pain
  +-> ectopic risk

---

PID vs ENDOMETRIOSIS

Same complications, but:
  PID = infection-triggered
  Endo = menstruation-triggered



CAUSES AND DIAGNOSIS

Main organisms:
  Neisseria gonorrhoeae
  Chlamydia trachomatis

Others:
  Gardnerella, Mycoplasma,
  TB, E. coli, etc.

---

Differentials:
  - appendicitis
  - ectopic pregnancy
  - twisted ovarian cyst
  - septic abortion
  - pyelonephritis

---

Investigations:

Blood
  +-- acute: WBC up, CRP up
  +-- chronic: ESR up
Urine culture
PCR / NAAT
Ultrasound
  |
  v

  |
  v

visualization + sampling)
