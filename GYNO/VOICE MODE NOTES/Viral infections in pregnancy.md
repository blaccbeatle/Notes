INFECTIONS IN PREGNANCY
=======================



HSV IN PREGNANCY

Neonatal route
  Infected birth canal
    |
    v
  Baby infected at birth
  (HSV rarely crosses placenta)

Couple
  HSV found
    |
    v
  Refer to STI clinic
  Screen syphilis + HIV

  |
  +-- PRIMARY (no antibodies)
  |     |

  |
  +-- RECURRENT (antibodies)
        |
        v

  |     |

  |
  +-- Early primary (4-6+ wks)
  |     |

  |

    |

VZV (CHICKENPOX)

Two forms
  |
  +-- Primary = CHICKENPOX
  |     whole body blisters
  |
  +-- Reactivation = SHINGLES
        one dermatome
        more painful (nerve)

---

Spread
  Airborne droplets
  + vesicular fluid contact
    |
    v
  Incubation 10-21 days
    |
    v
  Infectious: 2 days before
  rash until all crusted
  (about 5-6 days)

---

Pregnancy risk
  Relative immunosuppression
    |
    v
  Dissemination more likely
    |
    v
  Varicella pneumonia
  (about 30% mortality)

---

Three fetal/neonatal risks
  |
  +-- Congenital varicella
  |     syndrome
  |       |
  |       +-- under 13 wks: high
  |       +-- 13-20 wks: peak ~2%
  |       +-- after 20 wks: v low
  |       +-- after 28 wks: none
  |
  +-- Severe maternal
  |     dissemination
  |
  +-- Neonatal varicella
        |
        v
      Worst if rash is 5 days
      before to 2 days after
      delivery (high virus,
      few antibodies)



VZV MANAGEMENT

Exposed, no symptoms
  |
  v
History of chickenpox?
  |
  +-- Yes -> immune, reassure
  |
  +-- Unsure -> test IgG
        |
        +-- Positive -> reassure
        |
        +-- Negative -> VZIG
              |
              v
            Ideally within 72 hrs
            Works up to 10 days
            Protects ~3 wks

---

Active infection (blisters)
  |
  +-- Under 20 wks
  |     |
  |     v
  |   No acyclovir, observe
  |   Mother has complications?
  |     -> give acyclovir
  |
  +-- 20 wks or more
        |
        v
      Acyclovir 800 mg x5/day
      5-7 days
      Best within 24 hrs

---

Near term (e.g. 38 wks)
  Blisters appear
    |
    v
  Delay delivery 5+ days
  (antibody transfer)
    |
    v
  Can't delay -> VAGINAL
    |
    v
  If cesarean needed:
    no spinal via back blisters
    no incision via belly blisters
    |
    v
  Newborn VZIG if high risk

---

Screening for CVS
  Early infection (e.g. 10 wks)
    |
    v
  From ~20 wks:
  ultrasound + amnio PCR
    |
    +-- Both positive
    |     -> high risk, confirmed
    |
    +-- One positive
          -> lower risk, monitor



HIV BASICS

Virus
  RNA virus
    |
    v
  Spread by:
    sex (vaginal/anal/oral)
    needle prick
    blood transfusion
    mother to child

---

HIV vs AIDS
  HIV infection
    |
    v
  CD4 falls over time
    |
    +-- CD4 200 or more
    |     -> HIV only
    |
    +-- CD4 under 200
          -> AIDS

---

Mother-to-child (MTCT)
  |
  +-- Transplacental
  |     (higher at delivery)
  |
  +-- Intrapartum
  |     (blood, secretions)
  |
  +-- Breast milk
        (~30% of cases)
        -> no breastfeeding

---

Key point
  High maternal viral load
  = main MTCT risk
    |
    v
  Pregnant: always HAART
  (never monotherapy)



HIV MANAGEMENT

When to start HAART
  (avoid first 12 wks)
  Viral load (copies/mL)
    |
    +-- over 100,000
    |     -> start immediately
    |
    +-- 10,000-100,000
    |     -> after ~16 wks
    |
    +-- under 10,000
    |     -> after ~20 wks
    |
    +-- undetectable
          -> still start

  Once started: lifelong

  Mother refuses HAART
    |
    v
  Baby still gets zidovudine

---

Delivery mode
  Viral load at 36 wks
    |
    +-- under 50
    |     -> VAGINAL
    |
    +-- 50-400
    |     -> grey area
    |        lean CESAREAN
    |
    +-- over 400
    |     -> CESAREAN
    |
    +-- on zidovudine alone
          -> CESAREAN always

  Refuses cesarean
    -> vaginal after counselling

---

In labour
  IV zidovudine
  Start 3+ hrs before delivery

  Status unknown?
    |
    v
  Rapid test
    |
    v
  Positive, no time to confirm
    -> IV zidovudine
    -> vaginal or cesarean

---

PROM
  No wait-and-see
    |
    +-- Viral load over 400
    |     -> immediate cesarean
    |
    +-- Viral load 50-400
    |     -> consider cesarean
    |
    +-- Viral load under 50
          -> immediate induction

  By gestation
    |
    +-- 34 wks or more
    |     -> deliver immediately
    |
    +-- Under 34 wks
          -> erythromycin
          -> monitor
          -> aim past 34 wks
