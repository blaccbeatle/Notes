INFECTIONS IN PREGNANCY
=======================

HSV

HSV-2 = genital STI
  |
  v
Refer couple to STI clinic
Screen for syphilis + HIV

---

BABY RISK

Main risk = birth canal
(HSV rarely crosses placenta)
  |
  v
Neonatal HSV: high mortality
  - conjunctivitis
  - mucous membranes
  - pneumonia
  - meningitis / CNS

---

TYPE OF INFECTION

Primary or recurrent?
  |
  +-- PRIMARY (no antibodies)
  |     transmission 30-50%
  |
  +-- RECURRENT (antibodies)
        transmission 1-2%

Antibodies need 4-6 wks
to cross to baby

---

DELIVERY

Primary in 3rd trimester?
  |
  +-- YES -> CESAREAN
  |
  +-- NO -> VAGINAL
      (recurrent, or early
       primary with 4-6+ wks)

---

DRUGS

Active primary:
  acyclovir 400 mg TDS
  (or 200 mg 5x/day)
  for 5-10 days

From 36 wks to delivery:
  acyclovir 400 mg TDS
  (suppression)



VZV CLINICAL

Primary VZV = CHICKENPOX
  (all over body)
Reactivation = ZOSTER
  (one dermatome, more painful)

---

SPREAD

Airborne + vesicle fluid
  |
  v
Incubation 10-21 days
  |
  v
Infectious from 2 days
BEFORE rash until ALL
lesions crust

---

PREGNANCY RISKS

Pregnancy = relative
immunosuppression
  |
  v
Disseminated disease
Pneumonia ~30% mortality
  |
  v
3 risks to baby/pregnancy:
  1 congenital varicella
  2 severe maternal disease
  3 neonatal varicella

---

CONGENITAL VARICELLA

Gestation at infection:
  <13 wks   highest risk
  13-20 wks peak (~2%)
  >20 wks   very low
  >28 wks   none

Features:
  nasal + limb hypoplasia
  microcephaly
  skin scarring
  eye / CNS defects

---

NEONATAL VARICELLA

Maternal rash from
5 days BEFORE to
2 days AFTER delivery
  |
  v
High viral load + few
antibodies = SEVERE



VZV MANAGEMENT

EXPOSED, NO SYMPTOMS

History of chickenpox?
  |
  +-- YES -> immune, reassure
  |
  +-- UNSURE -> test IgG
        |
        +-- positive -> reassure
        |
        +-- negative -> VZIG
              best within 72 h
              works up to 10 d
              protects ~3 wks

Vaccinate postpartum

---

ACTIVE BLISTERS

Gestation?
  |
  +-- <20 wks
  |     observe, no acyclovir
  |     unless maternal
  |     complications -> give
  |
  +-- >=20 wks
        acyclovir 800 mg
        5x/day, 5-7 days
        best within 24 h

---

NEAR TERM (e.g. 38 WKS)

Blisters appear
  |
  v
Delay delivery >=5 days
(antibody transfer)
  |
  v
Vaginal preferred
(chickenpox alone is not
 a cesarean indication)

If cesarean needed:
  - no spinal needle through
    back blisters
  - no incision through
    abdominal blisters

Newborn VZIG if high risk
Breastfeeding ok

---

SCREEN FOR CVS

From ~20 wks:
ultrasound + amnio PCR
  |
  +-- both positive -> HIGH risk
  |
  +-- one positive -> monitor



HIV BASICS

RNA virus
Spread: sexual, blood
(needles, transfusion),
mother-to-child

---

HIV VS AIDS

HIV infection
  |
  v
CD4 falls
  |
  v
CD4 < 200 = AIDS

---

DIAGNOSIS + TREATMENT

ELISA -> Western blot
  |
  v
Pregnant? -> always HAART
(never monotherapy)

---

MOTHER-TO-CHILD SPREAD

3 routes:
  - transplacental
    (higher at delivery)
  - intrapartum
  - breast milk (~30%)
    -> NO breastfeeding

Key risk factor:
HIGH MATERNAL VIRAL LOAD

---

SCREENING

At booking: HIV + syphilis
  |
  v
If positive: confirm, then
  - viral load
  - CD4 count
  - resistance testing
  |
  v
Repeat each trimester
and at 36 wks



HIV MANAGEMENT

WHEN TO START HAART

Avoid first ~12 wks
(organogenesis)
  |
  v
Viral load (copies/mL)?
  |
  +-- >100,000 -> immediately
  +-- 10,000-100,000
  |     -> after ~16 wks
  +-- <10,000 -> after ~20 wks
  +-- undetectable -> still
        start

HAART is lifelong
Mother refuses?
  -> baby gets zidovudine

---

DELIVERY PLAN

Plan at 38 wks
Tertiary centre
  |
  v
Viral load at 36 wks?
  |
  +-- <50 -> VAGINAL
  +-- 50-400 -> consider
  |     CESAREAN (grey area)
  +-- >400 -> CESAREAN
  +-- on zidovudine alone
        -> CESAREAN

Refuses cesarean?
  -> vaginal after counselling

---

IN LABOUR

IV zidovudine
start >=3 h before delivery
  |
  v
Status unknown?
  |
  v
Rapid test
  |
  v
Positive + no time to
confirm -> IV zidovudine,
then vaginal or cesarean

---

PROM

No conservative management
  |
  v
Gestation?
  |
  +-- >=34 wks
  |     deliver immediately
  |     (single-dose
  |      dexamethasone if
  |      cesarean)
  |
  +-- <34 wks
        erythromycin, monitor,
        aim for delivery
        after 34 wks

Mode by viral load:
  >400    -> cesarean
  50-400  -> consider cesarean
  <50     -> induce, vaginal
