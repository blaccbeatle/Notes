Booking Visit
Booking visit (6–8 wks)
   │
   ▼
History: LMP→EDD, planned?, folic acid, rubella,
obstetric/medical/family/social, last contraception
   │
   ├─ Abdominal pain ───► ectopic? ──┐
   ├─ PV bleeding ──────► miscarriage/ectopic? ─┤
   │                     (no VE, no speculum)   │
   │                                            ▼
   │                               IMMEDIATE ADMISSION
   ├─ Urinary symptoms ─► rule out UTI → treat
   │
   ▼
Examination
   ├─ Height <145 cm → plan LSCS + LBW risk
   ├─ BMI → at-risk classification
   └─ Pallor, oedema, BP, heart/lung, abdomen, breast
   │
   ▼
Investigations
   ├─ FBC (Hb + thalassaemia)
   │     └─ MCV <80 or MCH <27 → refer
   ├─ FBS or PPBS (not both)
   ├─ VDRL + HIV Ab 1 & 2
   ├─ Blood group + Rh
   └─ Urine dipstick (full report → hospital only)



Routine Antenatal Visits, Screening & Scans
Every visit ─► Wt (0.1 kg), pallor, BP graph,
               abdomen + SFH, urine sugar + protein
               (no dipstick → Benedict's / heat precipitation)

Booking only ─► Ht (0.5 cm), BMI, respiratory, breast

Each trimester ─► cardiac auscultation, mental health

2nd trimester ─► dental assessment → dental surgeon
---
HIV + VDRL at booking (all)
   │
   ├─ Positive ─► STD clinic → TPHA/TPPA → treat
   │
   └─ Negative
        ├─ Low risk ─► no repeat
        └─ High risk ─► repeat at 28–32 weeks
---
Scans (hospital only)
Dating 9–11 wks → Anomaly 18–21 wks → Growth ~34 wks
                                       (PIH, GDM, IUGR,
                                        oligohydramnios)

PIH Spectrum
Raised BP in pregnancy
   │
   ▼
Simple PIH
   ├─ Frequent BP monitoring
   ├─ Antihypertensive compliance
   ├─ Kick count chart
   └─ Educate red flags
   │
   │ Worsening oedema / frothy urine /
   │ headache / vomiting / visual disturbance
   ▼
Pre-eclampsia
   ├─ ADMIT → Base Hospital Type B+
   ├─ Recheck urine albumin + BP
   ├─ IV antihypertensives considered
   ├─ MgSO₄ if impending eclampsia
   └─ Termination decision (registrar/consultant;
      fetal lung maturity)
   │
   │ Seizures
   ▼
Eclampsia
   ├─ At MOH: left lateral → O₂ → transfer
   │          (MOH staff accompany; no MgSO₄ at MOH)
   └─ At hospital: seizure control → terminate ASAP



Supplements & Anaemia
Pre-conception ─► Folic acid 1 mg (→ end of 3rd trimester)
      │
1st trimester ─► Folic acid + 
      │          Thriposha 2 packets/month
      │          (no iron unless indicated)
      ▼
2nd trimester: Hb status?
      │
      ├─ Non-anaemic
      │    ├─ Iron 30 mg + folic acid 0.4 mg
      │    ├─ Vit C 100 mg
      │    ├─ Calcium lactate 300 mg
      │    └─ MMN 1/day
      │
      └─ Anaemic (Hb <11 booking / <10.5 at 24–28 wks)
           ├─ Iron 60 mg BD (120 mg/day) × 3 months
           ├─ Vit C 100 mg + calcium lactate
           │
           ▼
        Recheck Hb at 3 months
           ├─ Improving → 3 more months → ↓ 30 mg/day
           └─ Not improving → specialist → IV iron
      │
      ▼
Delivery ─► all supplements continue 6 months postpartum

Delivery & Pre-Discharge
Previous CS?
   ├─ 1 → VBAC possible → Base Hospital Type B+
   └─ 2 → repeat CS
   │
   ▼
Delivery (target 100% institutional; current >99%)
   │
   ├─ Baby: cry → dry → clamp/cut cord → Apgar 1, 5, 10 min
   │        → Wt, length, OFC → Vit K
   │
   └─ Mother: suture → placenta check → fundal massage
              → breastfeed within 30 min – 1 h
   │
   ▼
Postnatal ward (intern exam, all mothers)
   ├─ Ask: bleeding, fever, abdominal pain
   └─ Check: BP, temp, uterus, lochia, scars
   │
   ▼
Pre-discharge
   ├─ Baby: BCG (≤24 h), heel prick, full exam → CHDR
   └─ Mother: Vit A 200,000 IU, rubella, anti-D (if needed),
              VE for retained gauze, BP
   │
   ▼
Discharge: NVD 24 h | assisted 24–48 h | LSCS 48 h

Family Planning
Couple needs contraception
   │
   ▼
Child wanted within?
   ├─ ≤1 year ─► condoms OK; any temporary except DMPA
   ├─ ≥2 years ─► longer-acting temporary (not condoms)
   └─ Family complete ─► check LRT/vasectomy eligibility
   │
   ▼
Medical condition?
   ├─ Migraine / controlled HTN / active PIH ─► no COCP
   ├─ IHD / multiple CV risks / chronic DM ─► no COCP, no DMPA
   ├─ Breast cancer ─► no hormonal methods
   ├─ High BMI ─► no COCP, no Jadelle
   └─ Breastfeeding ─► no COCP (POP safe)
   │
   ▼
Where?
   ├─ Home (PHM) ─► COCP, condoms only
   ├─ MOH ─► all except POP, Mirena, permanent
   └─ FHB ─► all, free (Mirena unavailable)
---
LRT eligibility
   ├─ ≥3 children ─────────────────► eligible
   ├─ 2 children, >26 yrs, youngest >2 yrs ─► eligible
   ├─ 2 children, <26 yrs ─────────► not eligible
   └─ 3rd CS ──────────────────────► must offer
   (spousal consent not required)
---
Postnatal start times
Condoms ─► immediately
Cu IUCD ─► ≤48 h, else 6 weeks
LRT ────► ≤7 days, else 6 weeks
COCP ───► 6 months
Others ─► 6 weeks
---
Unprotected intercourse
   ├─ ≤72 h ─► LNG 1.5 mg, or 0.75 mg × 2 (12 h apart)
   └─ ≤5 days ─► Cu IUCD
---
Started COCP/implant/DMPA
   ├─ Day 1–5 of cycle ─► immediate protection
   └─ After day 5 ─────► barrier 7 days

Missed COCP
   ├─ 1 pill ─► take ASAP, continue
   └─ ≥2 pills ─► barrier 7 days
        └─ <7 hormonal pills left → skip placebo week



Maternal, Fetal & Infant Death
Death of woman 15–50 yrs, pregnant or ≤1 year after
   │
   ▼
Staff inform head of institution immediately
   │
   ▼
Head notifies within 24 h (phone → letter)
   → MOH, RDHS, PDHS, Director FHB, previous institution
   │
   ▼
Investigation (head leads) → H677 within 14 days
   → copies: head, RDHS, Director FHB
   │
   ▼
Purpose: identify errors → correct practice
(not legal action; homicide handled separately)
   │
   ▼
Counts for MMR?
   ├─ ≤42 days, non-accidental ─► yes
   └─ 6 weeks – 1 year / accidental ─► notify only
---
Three delays (identify from scenario, not order)
1st: deciding to seek care ─────┐
2nd: reaching adequate facility ─┼─► maternal death
3rd: receiving care (≤20 min) ───┘
