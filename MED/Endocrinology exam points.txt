  |            loop diuretics

4. THYROID PHYSIOLOGY + TFTS

Hypothalamus -> TRH
  |
  v
Anterior pituitary -> TSH
  |
  v
Thyroid -> mainly T4 (some T3)
  |
  v
Peripheral T4 -> T3 (active)
  -> nuclear receptor
  |
T4 + T3 -> negative feedback
on TRH + TSH

---

Binding
  T4 ~99.95%
    ~67% TBG
    ~20% transthyretin
    ~13% albumin
  T3 ~99.8% (mostly TBG)
  Only free T4/T3 active
  TBG rises in pregnancy
  Normal TSH: 0.4 - 4.0 mIU/L

---

TFT pattern
  |
  |-- T4 hi, T3 hi, TSH lo
  |     -> hyperthyroidism
  |-- T4 N, T3 hi, TSH lo
  |     -> T3 toxicosis
  |-- T4 N, T3 N, TSH lo
  |     -> subclinical hyper
  |-- T4 lo, T3 lo, TSH hi
  |     -> primary hypothyroid
  |-- T4 N, T3 N, TSH mild hi
  |     -> subclinical hypo
  |-- T4 lo, T3 lo,
  |   TSH lo/inappropriate N
  |     -> central hypothyroid
  |-- T4 lo, T3 very lo,
  |   TSH N/lo, acutely ill
  |     -> sick euthyroid
  '-- On thyroxine: T4 N/hi,
      TSH hi
        -> poor compliance
           (dose just before
           test)
