RHEUMATOLOGY: CONSOLIDATED FLOWCHARTS
=====================================



1. ARTHRITIS BASICS

Joint pain
  |
  +--> Inflammatory
  |      +-- Pathology: synovitis
  |      +-- AM stiffness: yes
  |      +-- Worse rest, better activity
  |      +-- RA, seronegative, crystal,
  |          post-viral
  |
  +--> Mechanical (e.g. OA)
         +-- No significant inflammation
         +-- AM stiffness: no/minimal
         +-- Worse activity, better rest
         +-- Osteoarthritis

---

Joint count
  |
  +--> Mono (1 joint)
  |      +-- Common: gout, pseudogout,
  |      |   septic arthritis
  |      +-- Occasional: psoriatic,
  |          reactive, RA
  |
  +--> Poly (>1 joint)
         +-- RA, reactive, seronegative,
             post-viral, enteropathic

---

DIP involvement
  |
  v
Almost never RA
  |
  v
Consider PsA or OA

RA + PsA -> raised CAD risk
(similar to diabetes)

---

Infection-associated arthritis
  +-- Rubella, chlamydial urethritis,
  |   chikungunya
  +-- Hep B -> immune-complex
      arthritis (prodromal phase)



2. RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS

Bilateral, symmetrical polyarthritis
  +-- MCP + PIP; DIP spared
  +-- F > M; HLA-DR4; age 30-50
  +-- Synovitis -> pannus

---

Serology
  |
  +--> RF (IgM vs Fc of IgG)
  |      +-- Present: does not confirm
  |      +-- Absent: does not exclude
  |
  +--> Anti-CCP
         +-- More specific
         +-- Predicts erosions,
             worse prognosis

---

Deformities
  +-- Ulnar deviation, boutonniere,
  |   swan neck, Z-thumb
  +-- Ulnar styloid subluxation
  +-- Atlantoaxial subluxation
        |
        v
      Cord compression
        |
        v
      Quadriparesis

---

Extra-articular
  +-- Lung: Caplan nodules, effusion,
  |   fibrosis/ILD, obliterative
  |   bronchiolitis
  +-- Eye: scleritis, episcleritis,
  |   scleromalacia perforans
  +-- Kidney: AA amyloidosis
  |   -> nephrotic syndrome
  +-- Vasculitis -> mononeuritis
  |   multiplex
  +-- Neuro: carpal tunnel
  +-- Felty: RA + splenomegaly +
  |   neutropenia (+/- pancytopenia)
  +-- Anaemia: chronic disease;
      iron deficiency (NSAID/steroid
      -> GI bleed)

---

X-ray hands
  |
  v
Soft tissue swelling
  |
  v
Peri-articular osteoporosis
  |
  v
Instability + deformity

Monitoring score: DAS-28

---

Treatment
  |
  v
Start DMARD ASAP
  +-- Interim pain: diclofenac
  +-- Steroids: short bridge only
  |
  v
Methotrexate (1st line)
  +-- Never start 2 DMARDs together
  |
  v
Not responding
  |
  v
DMARD combination
  |
  v
Uncontrolled on 2 DMARDs
  |
  v
Exclude TB (incl. latent)
  |
  v
Biologic: anti-TNF-alpha
  +-- Risk: raised lymphoma

---

DMARDs
  |
  +--> Methotrexate
  |      +-- Inhibits DHF reductase
  |      +-- Once weekly; folic acid
  |      |   on a different day
  |      +-- GI, hepatotoxicity
  |      +-- Marrow suppression
  |      |   (macrocytic anaemia)
  |      +-- Pneumonitis -> fibrosis
  |      +-- Oral/skin ulcers
  |      +-- Monitor FBC
  |      +-- CI in pregnancy
  |
  +--> Sulfasalazine
  |      +-- GI, leukopenia,
  |          marrow suppression
  |
  +--> Hydroxychloroquine
  |      +-- Less effective, fewer SE
  |      +-- Retinal toxicity
  |          -> regular eye checks
  |
  +--> Leflunomide
         +-- Blocks pyrimidine synthesis
         +-- GI, liver toxicity
         +-- CI in pregnancy

---

MTX + fever + sore throat
  |
  v
Suspect neutropenic sepsis
  +-- Admit
  +-- Stop MTX
  +-- FBC
  +-- Reverse barrier nursing
  +-- Folinic acid (NOT folic acid)
  +-- Consider G-CSF
  +-- Antibiotics + blood cultures

---

Biologics
  +-- Anti-TNF-alpha: infliximab,
  |   adalimumab, etanercept
  +-- IL-1: anakinra
  +-- Anti-CD20: rituximab

---

2010 ACR/EULAR (score >=6 = RA)
  |
  +--> Joints (max 5)
  |      +-- 1 large ............ 0
  |      +-- 2-10 large ......... 1
  |      +-- 1-3 small .......... 2
  |      +-- 4-10 small ......... 3
  |      +-- 10 (>=1 small) ..... 5
  |
  +--> Serology (max 3)
  |      +-- Negative ........... 0
  |      +-- Low positive ....... 2
  |      +-- High positive ...... 3
  |
  +--> ESR/CRP (max 1)
  |      +-- Normal ............. 0
  |      +-- Abnormal ........... 1
  |
  +--> Duration (max 1)
         +-- <6 weeks ........... 0
         +-- >=6 weeks .......... 1

Small: MCP, PIP, MTP 2-5,
       thumb IP, wrist
NOT:   DIP, 1st CMC, 1st MTP



3. SERONEGATIVE SPONDYLOARTHROPATHIES

RF neg + anti-CCP neg
  +-- HLA-B27
  +-- Enthesitis
  +-- Sacroiliitis
  +-- Typically asymmetrical
  +-- Uveitis/iritis: painful
      -> visual impairment

---

Ankylosing spondylitis
  +-- Axial (spine + SI); M >> F
  +-- Onset 16-40; >80% before 30
  +-- Back pain: worse morning /
  |   2nd half of night; better
  |   with exercise
  +-- Reduced neck extension/rotation
  +-- Reduced chest expansion
  |   (costochondritis + fibrosis)
  +-- Achilles tendinitis,
  |   plantar fasciitis
  +-- Peripheral: lower limb, large
      joints, asymmetrical

---

Suspected AS
  |
  v
Best test: HLA-B27
  |
  v
MRI SI joints/spine (key imaging;
early sacroiliitis)
  |
  v
X-ray: erosions
  |
  v
Syndesmophytes
  |
  v
Bamboo spine

Schober's test: spinal stiffness

---

AS treatment
  +-- Exercise, NSAIDs
  +-- Anti-TNF: persistent activity
  +-- MTX: peripheral, NOT spinal

AS complications
  +-- Aortic regurgitation
  +-- Heart block
  +-- Apical fibrosis

---

Psoriatic arthritis
  +-- DIP; M = F
  +-- HLA-B27 less strong
  +-- Dactylitis
  +-- Nails: pitting, onycholysis,
  |   oil drop sign
  +-- Arthritis mutilans
  |   -> telescopic fingers
  +-- X-ray: pencil-in-cup
  +-- Check scalp, umbilicus,
  |   natal cleft
  +-- Poor skin-joint correlation
  +-- MTX effective; anti-TNF
      (skin + joints)

---

Infection (1-4 weeks before)
  |
  +--> Dysentery: Yersinia,
  |    Salmonella, Shigella,
  |    Campylobacter
  |
  +--> Urethritis (NSU):
       Chlamydia, Ureaplasma
  |
  v
REACTIVE ARTHRITIS
  +-- Triad: urethritis + arthritis
  |   + conjunctivitis
  +-- Asymmetric lower-limb
  |   oligoarthritis
  +-- Knee, ankle, MTP; dactylitis
  +-- Plantar fasciitis, Achilles
  +-- Keratoderma blennorrhagica
  +-- Circinate balanitis
  +-- Erythema nodosum-like lesions
  +-- Serositis
  +-- Non-destructive; usually
      single attack

---

IBD (UC/Crohn's) + arthritis
  |
  v
ENTEROPATHIC ARTHRITIS
  +-- Asymmetrical, large joints
  +-- May precede bowel symptoms
  +-- Colectomy: helps UC > Crohn's



4. CRYSTAL ARTHRITIS

Acute monoarthritis
  |
  v
Joint aspiration (essential)
  |
  +--> Needle, negative
  |    birefringence -> GOUT
  |
  +--> Rhomboid, weakly positive
  |    birefringence -> PSEUDOGOUT
  |
  +--> Pus cells >50,000 -> SEPTIC

---

Gout
  +-- 1st MTP (podagra)
  +-- Causes: reduced renal excretion
  |   (most), metabolic syndrome,
  |   thiazides, low-dose aspirin,
  |   alcohol, polycythaemia rubra
  |   vera, CKD
  +-- Polyarticular / chronic
  |   tophaceous gout <-> CKD
  +-- Uric acid normal/low in acute
  |   attack (low != exclude,
  |   high != confirm)
  +-- X-ray: punched-out erosions,
      overhanging edges, preserved
      joint space

---

Gout treatment
  |
  +--> Acute
  |      +-- NSAIDs
  |      +-- Colchicine (diarrhoea)
  |      +-- Steroids
  |
  +--> Long-term (attacks, tophi,
       stones, CKD)
         +-- Allopurinol, febuxostat,
         |   probenecid
         +-- Do NOT start in acute
             attack

---

Pseudogout (CPPD)
  +-- Elderly
  +-- Young: haemochromatosis,
  |   hyperparathyroidism, Wilson's,
  |   hypomagnesaemia, alkaptonuria
  +-- Knee/wrist
  +-- X-ray: chondrocalcinosis
  +-- Tx: aspiration, NSAIDs,
      colchicine, IA steroids



5. SEPTIC ARTHRITIS

Hot joint
  |
  v
Joint aspiration (Gram stain +
culture) = investigation of choice
  |
  v
Pus cells >50,000 -> septic
  +-- Most common: Staph aureus
  +-- Pustular rash + arthritis
      -> gonococcal (oligoarticular)



6. SLE

Profile
  +-- Females; adolescents/young
  |   adults (peak 20-40)
  +-- Drugs: hydralazine,
  |   procainamide, penicillamine
  +-- Most common: arthralgia, rash
  +-- Most serious: kidney, brain

---

Clinical
  +-- Malar rash (spares nasolabial
  |   folds); episcleritis
  +-- Raynaud's: 20-28%
  +-- Joints: non-erosive;
  |   Jaccoud's arthropathy
  +-- AVN hip (high-dose steroids)
  +-- Pericarditis: worse lying,
  |   relieved leaning forward
  +-- Libman-Sacks endocarditis
  +-- Renal: nephritic, nephrotic,
      AKI, CKD

---

Blood
  +-- Predominant lymphopenia
  +-- Warm AIHA -> microspherocytes
  +-- Thrombocytopenia
  +-- CTD + pancytopenia -> SLE

---

SLE + altered consciousness
  |
  +--> Cerebral lupus (assoc. APLS)
  +--> Uraemic encephalopathy
  +--> + papilloedema -> CVT
  +--> On immunosuppressants
       -> infection

---

Lupus nephritis classes
  1  Minimal mesangial
  2  Mesangial proliferative
  3  Focal proliferative
  4  Diffuse proliferative
  5  Membranous
  6  Advanced sclerosing

---

Labs
  +-- ESR raised
  +-- CRP normal, even in flare
  |   -> rise = arthritis/infection
  +-- C3/C4 reduced in flare
  +-- ANA: almost always +,
  |   low specificity
  +-- Anti-dsDNA: very specific;
  |   sensitivity ~70%
  +-- Anti-Sm: low prevalence,
  |   very specific
  +-- Anti-Ro/La -> cross placenta
      -> fetal complete heart block

---

Flare
  +-- Raised ESR
  +-- Raised anti-dsDNA
  +-- Reduced C3/C4
  +-- Raised proteinuria (renal)

---

Diagnosis
  +-- ACR: 11 items, need >=4
  +-- SLICC: >=4 (>=1 clinical +
  |   >=1 immunologic)
  +-- ANA + biopsy-proven lupus
      nephritis

---

Management
  +-- Sun avoidance; reduce CV risk
  +-- Hydroxychloroquine for ALL
  |   (monitor eyes; reduced
  |   thrombosis, better pregnancy,
  |   CV protection)
  +-- Lupus nephritis
  |     |
  |     v
  |   IV pulse methylprednisolone
  |   (1st line)
  |     |
  |     v
  |   Alternative: cyclophosphamide
  +-- Major organ: steroids,
  |   mycophenolate, rituximab
  +-- MTX: can use, not 1st line

---

Pregnancy
  +-- Not absolute contraindication
  +-- Control disease >=3 months
  |   before conception
  +-- Most drugs safe EXCEPT
      cyclophosphamide



7. ANTIPHOSPHOLIPID SYNDROME

Clinical criterion (>=1)
  +-- Arterial/venous thrombosis
  +-- >=1 fetal death >=10 weeks
  +-- >=1 premature birth
  |   <34 weeks
  +-- >=3 consecutive losses
      <20 weeks
        +
Lab criterion (>=2 tests,
12 weeks apart)
  +-- Lupus anticoagulant
  +-- Anti-cardiolipin
  +-- Anti-beta-2 glycoprotein I
        |
        v
      APLS (primary or 2nd to SLE)
        +-- Low platelets
        +-- Prolonged aPTT
        +-- False-positive VDRL
        +-- Tx: warfarin or heparin



8. OVERLAP SYNDROMES

2 CTDs in one patient
  +-- RA + SLE (most common)
  +-- Scleroderma + SLE
  +-- Scleroderma + myositis
  +-- Scleroderma + RA
  +-- Any + Sjogren's

---

MCTD (SLE + scleroderma +
polymyositis)
  +-- Swollen hands
  +-- Synovitis, myositis
  +-- Raynaud's, acrosclerosis
  +-- Anti-U1-RNP (high titer)



9. SJOGREN'S SYNDROME

Dry eyes + dry mouth (+ vagina)
  |
  +--> Primary (alone)
  +--> Secondary (RA, SLE)
  |
  v
Tests
  +-- Schirmer's test
  +-- RF usually +; ANA ~80%
  +-- Anti-Ro / anti-La
  +-- Best confirm: labial gland
      biopsy

---

Associations
  +-- RTA, DI, lung fibrosis
  +-- Raised non-Hodgkin's lymphoma

Tx: artificial tears, saliva
substitutes, hydroxychloroquine



10. SYSTEMIC SCLEROSIS

Skin tightening
  |
  +--> LIMITED (CREST)
  |      +-- Not beyond MCP joints
  |      +-- Raynaud's early
  |      +-- Pulmonary HTN early
  |      +-- Anti-centromere
  |
  +--> DIFFUSE
         +-- Beyond elbows/knees
         |   + trunk
         +-- Raynaud's later
         +-- Lung fibrosis
         |   -> pulm HTN later
         +-- GIT (diarrhoea), heart,
         |   kidneys
         +-- Anti-Scl-70

CREST = Calcinosis, Raynaud's,
Esophageal dysmotility,
Sclerodactyly, Telangiectasia

ANA ~95% (both); RF ~30%
Raynaud's: more than in SLE

---

Reduced urine output + renal
failure + high BP
  |
  v
Renal crisis
  |
  v
ACE inhibitor

---

Symptom treatment
  +-- Raynaud's: nifedipine
  +-- Oesophagus: domperidone + PPI
  +-- Pulm HTN: prostaglandin
  |   analogs
  +-- Fibrosis: steroids +
      cyclophosphamide



11. POLYMYOSITIS / DERMATOMYOSITIS

Proximal symmetrical weakness
  |
  v
Raised CK + EMG + biopsy
  |
  +--> Muscle only -> POLYMYOSITIS
  |      +-- Endomysial infiltrate
  |
  +--> + skin -> DERMATOMYOSITIS
         +-- Perifascicular
         +-- Stronger malignancy link
         |   (gastric, ovarian)
         +-- Screen at diagnosis

Tx: steroids; MTX, azathioprine,
mycophenolate

---

Antisynthetase syndrome
  +-- Myositis
  +-- Mechanic's hands, Raynaud's
  +-- Lung fibrosis
  +-- Non-erosive arthritis
  +-- Anti-Jo-1

---

New weakness on steroids
  |
  v
Check CK / stop steroid briefly
  |
  +--> CK high, worsens
  |      -> RELAPSE
  |      -> increase steroids
  |
  +--> CK normal, improves
         -> STEROID MYOPATHY
         -> reduce/stop steroids

  |      +-- 10-15% have GCA

Large vessel vasculitis:

  |   (thousands)

---

Child + purpura legs/buttocks
  |
  v
HSP
  +-- No anaemia/thrombocytopenia
  +-- IgA nephropathy-like
  +-- Type III hypersensitivity
  +-- Mesenteric vasculitis
  |   -> intussusception
  +-- Diagnosis: clinical

---

Carpal tunnel
  +-- RA complication
  +-- Exclude hypothyroidism
  +-- Worse at night
  +-- Thenar wasting
  +-- Tx: nocturnal splinting first

---

Misc
  +-- Behcet's: oral + genital
  |   ulcers; arterial + venous
  |   thrombosis
  +-- Tietze: costochondritis
  +-- Raynaud's: beta blockers,
  |   vibrating tools, cervical rib
  +-- Long bone fracture + upper
      limb rash -> fat embolism

---

Back pain

Red flags? (fever, weight loss,

  |
  v
Start with X-ray
(not everyone needs MRI)



13. OSTEOARTHRITIS

Cartilage loss
  |
  v
Bone-on-bone
  +-- Subchondral sclerosis
  +-- Osteophytes
  +-- Subchondral cysts

---

Clinical
  +-- Pain with activity,
  |   better with rest
  +-- AM stiffness <30 min
  +-- Gelling, crepitus,
  |   mild effusion
  +-- Heberden's = DIP
  +-- Bouchard's = PIP
  +-- Knee: medial compartment
  +-- TMJ: not common
  +-- Obesity = risk;
      osteoporosis = protective

---

Investigations
  +-- ESR normal; RF/ANA negative
  +-- X-ray: space narrowing,
      sclerosis, osteophytes

---

Management
  |
  v
Weight loss (most important)
+ exercise
  |
  v
Analgesics / NSAIDs
  |
  v
IA steroids (flares)
  |
  v
Joint replacement (severe)



14. OSTEOPOROSIS

Osteoclasts > osteoblasts
  |
  v
Fragility fractures
  +-- Colles' (dinner fork)
  +-- Vertebral (kyphosis,
  |   reduced height)
  +-- NOF intracapsular -> AVN

Labs: Ca, PO4, ALP all normal

---

Risk factors
  +-- Oestrogen deficiency /
  |   early menopause
  +-- Steroids, heparin, PPIs,
  |   aromatase inhibitors
  +-- Loop diuretics
  +-- Excess caffeine
  +-- Low BMI, bed rest
  +-- Maternal hip fracture
  +-- NOT thiazides
  +-- Obesity protective

---

Clinical risk
  |
  v
FRAX (ages 40-90; 10-yr risk)
  |
  v
DEXA if indicated
  +-- F >65, high FRAX,
  |   willing to treat
  +-- Fragility fracture,
  |   young female
  +-- Long-term steroids
  +-- Osteopenia on X-ray
  +-- NOT for worry alone
